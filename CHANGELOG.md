## v1.0.0

This first release organises my game screenshots into a site I can browse by world.

- A homepage with a featured Red Dead Redemption 2 frame and a selection of game worlds.
- A catalogue of 16 games, each with its own page, colours, and gallery.
- 176 personal screenshots with smaller previews and links to the full-size photographs.
- Layouts for desktop and mobile screens, keyboard-friendly navigation, and reduced-motion support.
- Checks for the catalogue, generated pages, galleries, and local links and images.

The Red Dead Redemption 2 gallery images have had their embedded black borders cropped. Catalogue covers follow the photographs' proportions to keep subjects such as Tower Bridge in view.
